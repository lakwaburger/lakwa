About the Cafe

The Lakwa Burger Cafe is the kitchen of Lakwa Motorcycle Tours in El Nido, Palawan. We keep it simple: beef and crispy chicken burgers on buns we bake ourselves, Edam cheese, and a sauce you'll only find here.
